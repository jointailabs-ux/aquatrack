## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Resolution

Following the independent code audit of Round 6, this remediation addresses and resolves all identified defects:

1. **Scrap-Yard Stock Conservation (Part 2):**
   - Outgoing movements from `SCRAP_YARD` are now strictly locked and validated against available net balance from `jar_custody_movements`.
   - Outgoing movements cannot exceed available scrap balance.
   - Jars in `DAMAGED_SCRAP` condition are strictly terminal and can never transition back to usable or clean stock.
   - Scrap write-offs are restricted to the business owner.
2. **Canonical Singleton Warehouse Identity (Part 3):**
   - Added database-level singleton enforcement (`uq_singleton_warehouse`, `chk_single_default_warehouse`) to ensure only one central depot exists.
   - All RPCs (`rpc_record_jar_movement`, `rpc_create_customer_order`, `rpc_execute_delivery_stop`, `rpc_cancel_customer_order`, `rpc_complete_pos_sale`) resolve and validate the canonical warehouse location ID, rejecting arbitrary or mismatched warehouse IDs.
3. **Mandatory Destination Relationship Validation (Part 4):**
   - Eliminated silent skips of balance updates.
   - Destination locations of type `CUSTOMER` must reference an active customer record in `public.customers`.
   - Destination locations of type `SUPPLIER` must reference an active supplier record in `public.suppliers`.
   - Missing business records trigger immediate transaction rollbacks with explicit error messages.
4. **Order Inventory Lifecycle & Reservation (Part 5):**
   - `rpc_create_customer_order` now deterministically locks `inventory_balances` rows (`ORDER BY product_id FOR UPDATE`) and checks available inventory ($\text{quantity\_on\_hand} - \text{quantity\_reserved} \ge \text{requested\_quantity}$).
   - Orders atomically increment `quantity_reserved` to prevent overselling by concurrent requests.
   - Delivery stop completion (`rpc_execute_delivery_stop`) deducts delivered quantities from `quantity_on_hand` and releases `quantity_reserved`.
   - Order cancellation (`rpc_cancel_customer_order`) and failed deliveries release reserved inventory back to available stock.
5. **Unforgeable Internal RPC Authorization Tokens (Part 6):**
   - Created a dedicated internal table `public.internal_rpc_auth_tokens`, completely inaccessible (`REVOKE ALL`) to all direct PostgREST and SQL callers.
   - Authorization tokens are generated by trusted `SECURITY DEFINER` functions and tied directly to `txid_current()` and `auth.uid()`.
   - Triggers `check_customer_privileged_updates` and `check_supplier_privileged_updates` enforce domain scopes (`JAR_CUSTODY_ONLY`, `CUSTOMER_ORDER_BALANCE_ONLY`, `DELIVERY_STOP_EXECUTION`, `PURCHASE_RECEIPT`).
   - Tokens cannot survive beyond the executing transaction.
6. **Strict Supplier Purchase Validation (Part 7):**
   - Mandatory purchase order reference, status check ($\ne$ `'CANCELLED'`), remaining receivable quantity check, and supplier ownership validation.
7. **Harden Local Database Safety Guard (Part 9):**
   - Centralized URL validator `validateDatabaseUrlSafety` and `assertLocalDatabaseUrl` in `src/lib/security/database-safety.ts` and `scripts/database-safety.mjs`.
   - Enforced inside all runner entry points before client instantiation.
   - Comprehensive unit test suite (`tests/unit/database-safety.test.ts`) verifies rejection of remote Supabase domains, trick hostnames, and invalid ports.
